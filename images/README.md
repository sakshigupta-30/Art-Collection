Artwork and about images are currently referenced from Unsplash URLs in js/app.js and about.html.
To go fully local: drop files here and swap the `image` values in js/app.js (and the <img src> in about.html) to e.g. images/artworks/silent-forest.jpg.
Export at ~1200px wide, 4:3, WebP or JPEG q70-80.
